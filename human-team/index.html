Fast replies are only the beginning.

Many patient conversations are simple. Some are not. Eden combines the speed of AI with a trained human layer for moments where context, empathy, or judgment matter.

⚡

HOW IT WORKS

One conversation. The right level of help.

  1. 1

    Patient reaches out

    Website chat, voice, missed calls, or another connected patient channel.

  2. 2

    AI responds immediately

    The patient gets an answer, not a queue. Intent and context are captured from the start.

  3. 3

    Human Team joins when needed

    A trained specialist can review, reassure, clarify, escalate, or continue the conversation.

  4. 4

    The next step is confirmed

    Booking, callback, clinic escalation, or follow-up is clearly recorded and handed over.
